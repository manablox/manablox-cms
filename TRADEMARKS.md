# Trademark policy

"Manablox", the Manablox logo and the Manablox wordmark are marks of the
Manablox project. This policy says how you may use them. It is separate from the
[software licence](./LICENSE): the licence covers the code, this covers the name.

The point of the policy is narrow. Anyone should be able to say truthfully what
software they run, write about it, teach it and build on it. Nobody should be
able to make a modified Manablox look like the original, or make their own
product look like it comes from us.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, site or service is built with, runs on, or is
  compatible with Manablox.
- Use the name in the title of an article, tutorial, video, course or talk about
  Manablox.
- Keep the name and logo where Manablox itself shows them, and use them to credit
  the project and link to it.
- Name an extension, integration or plugin so that it reads as a description
  rather than as a product of ours: "Acme Payments for Manablox" is fine,
  "Manablox Payments" is not.
- Use the logo unmodified to refer to the project, at a size and position that
  does not make it look like the mark of your own product.
- Use the name in a package name under your own npm scope, for example
  `@acme/manablox-payments`. The `@manablox` npm scope itself belongs to the
  project.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the name or logo as the name, or as part of the name, of your own product,
  service, company or domain.
- Use them in a way that suggests your product is published, endorsed,
  certified, sponsored or supported by the Manablox project.
- Use them for a modified version of Manablox, or for a fork. A fork must carry
  its own name. It may state, factually and not in its own name or logo, that it
  is derived from Manablox.
- Alter the logo, or use it as part of another mark or logo.
- Register the name or logo, or anything confusingly similar, as a trademark,
  company name or domain name.
- Use them on merchandise for sale.

## Forks

A fork is welcome, and the licence allows it. Give it a different name, use a
different logo, and do not present it as Manablox. Say where it came from as
plainly as you like: "a fork of Manablox" is an accurate statement of fact and
this policy does not restrict it.

## The logo in the admin and the X-Powered-By header

Manablox shows its logo and wordmark in the administration interface and names
itself in the `X-Powered-By` response header. Running Manablox as it ships,
modified or not, with those in place is a permitted use of the marks; it needs
no permission. The MIT licence lets you change that code. If you distribute a
modified version under its own name, as a fork, the rules for forks above apply.

## Questions

If a use is not clearly covered here, ask before shipping. Open an issue in the
[Manablox CMS repository](https://github.com/manablox/manablox-cms/issues). Permission for a specific use is
usually easy to give and is given in writing.
